# 1. Introduction

> **Metabolomics** is the comprehensive study of small-molecule metabolites in biological systems and provides a functional readout of cellular biochemical states. It involves the systematic detection, identification, and quantification of metabolites, including amino acids, lipids, carbohydrates, nucleotides, and other metabolic intermediates, using analytical platforms such as mass spectrometry (MS) and nuclear magnetic resonance (NMR) spectroscopy. The cellular metabolome is highly dynamic and reflects the integrated effects of genetic, epigenetic, environmental, and physiological factors. By characterizing metabolic alterations and pathway activity, metabolomics provides insights into cellular function, metabolic regulation, disease mechanisms, and the interactions between metabolism and other molecular processes.

# 3. Analysis

## [MetaboAnalyst](https://www.metaboanalyst.ca/)

> MetaboAnalyst is a web-based platform dedicated for comprehensive metabolomics data analysis, interpretation and integration with other omics data.
> - Zhiqiang Pang, Yao Lu, Guangyan Zhou, Fiona Hui, Lei Xu, Charles Viau, Aliya F Spigelman, Patrick E MacDonald, David S Wishart, Shuzhao Li, Jianguo Xia, MetaboAnalyst 6.0: towards a unified platform for metabolomics data processing, analysis and interpretation, Nucleic Acids Research, Volume 52, Issue W1, 5 July 2024, Pages W398–W406, https://doi.org/10.1093/nar/gkae253.

## [mzmine](https://github.com/mzmine/mzmine)

> mzmine is an open-source software for mass spectrometry data processing. The goals of the project is to provide a user-friendly, flexible and easily extendable software with a complete set of modules covering the entire MS data analysis workflow. Support includes liquid chromatography (LC), gas chromatography (GC), ion mobility spectrometry (IMS), MS imaging (e.g., MALDI), and most MS instruments.

## [GNPS](https://gnps.ucsd.edu/ProteoSAFe/static/gnps-splash.jsp)

> GNPS is a web-based mass spectrometry ecosystem that aims to be an open-access knowledge base for community-wide organization and sharing of raw, processed, or annotated fragmentation mass spectrometry data (MS/MS).
> - [GNPS Documentation GitHub Repository](https://github.com/CCMS-UCSD/GNPSDocumentation)
> - Wang, M., Carver, J., Phelan, V. et al. Sharing and community curation of mass spectrometry data with Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking. Nat Biotechnol 34, 828–837 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.3597.

## [SIRIUS](https://github.com/sirius-ms/sirius)

> SIRIUS is a java-based software framework for the analysis of LC-MS/MS data of metabolites and other "small molecules of biological interest".
> - Dührkop, K., Fleischauer, M., Ludwig, M. et al. SIRIUS 4: a rapid tool for turning tandem mass spectra into metabolite structure information. Nat Methods 16, 299–302 (2019). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-019-0344-8.

## [xcms](https://github.com/sneumann/xcms)

> The xcms R package provides functionality to efficiently preprocess LC-MS (as well as GC-MS and LC-MS/MS) data.

## [tidymass](https://github.com/tidymass/tidymass)

> TidyMass is a comprehensive computational framework for data processing and analysis of LC-MS data using tidyverse principles.
> - Shen, X., Yan, H., Wang, C. _et al._ TidyMass an object-oriented reproducible analysis framework for LC–MS data. _Nat Commun_ **13**, 4365 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32155-w.

## [Workflow4Metabolomics](https://github.com/workflow4metabolomics/workflow4metabolomics)

> The Workflow4Metabolomics, W4M in short, is a French infrastructure offering software tool processing, analyzing and annotating metabolomics data. It is based on the Galaxy platform.
> - Franck Giacomoni, Gildas Le Corguillé, Misharl Monsoor, Marion Landi, Pierre Pericard, Mélanie Pétéra, Christophe Duperier, Marie Tremblay-Franco, Jean-François Martin, Daniel Jacob, Sophie Goulitquer, Etienne A. Thévenot, Christophe Caron, Workflow4Metabolomics: a collaborative research infrastructure for computational metabolomics, Bioinformatics, Volume 31, Issue 9, May 2015, Pages 1493–1495, https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btu813.

# Database

> 1. [MetaboLights](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/metabolights/): MetaboLights is a database for Metabolomics experiments and derived information. The database is cross-species, cross-technique and covers metabolite structures and their reference spectra as well as their biological roles, locations and concentrations, and experimental data from metabolic experiments.
> 2. [Chemical Entities of Biological Interest, ChEBI](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/chebi/): A manually curated database and ontology of chemical entities.
> 3. [Reactome](https://reactome.org/): REACTOME is an open-source, open access, manually curated and peer-reviewed pathway database.
> 4. [Metabolomics Workbench](https://www.metabolomicsworkbench.org/): The NIH Common Fund's National Metabolomics Data Repository (NMDR) is now accepting metabolomics data for small and large studies on cells, tissues and organisms via the Metabolomics Workbench.
