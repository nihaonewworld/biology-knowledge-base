# Biology Protocols Resources

Selected websites for finding experimental protocols, technical guides, and methods literature in biology.

## Protocol Repositories and Discovery Platforms

- [protocols.io](https://www.protocols.io/) — Platform for sharing, discovering, and versioning research protocols.
- [Springer Nature Experiments](https://experiments.springernature.com/) — Search platform for protocols and methods from Springer Nature publications.
- [OpenWetWare Protocols](https://openwetware.org/wiki/Protocols) — Community wiki of laboratory protocols, reagent recipes, and practical notes.

## Protocol Journals and Book Series

- [Nature Protocols](https://www.nature.com/nprot/) — Peer-reviewed experimental protocols with detailed procedures and troubleshooting guidance.
- [Current Protocols](https://currentprotocols.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/) — Detailed protocols and practical guidance across life science disciplines.
- [Cold Spring Harbor Protocols](https://cshprotocols.cshlp.org/) — Laboratory protocols for molecular biology, genetics, cell biology, and related fields.
- [Bio-protocol](https://bio-protocol.org/en) — Open-access, peer-reviewed protocols across biological research fields.
- [STAR Protocols](https://www.cell.com/star-protocols/home) — Open-access, peer-reviewed protocols with expected outcomes, limitations, and troubleshooting.
- [Methods in Molecular Biology](https://link.springer.com/series/7651) — Book series of step-by-step protocols with reagent lists and practical notes.

## Video Protocols and Methods Journals

- [JoVE Research](https://www.jove.com/research) — Video demonstrations of research techniques, including a peer-reviewed video journal.
- [MethodsX](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/methodsx) — Open-access journal publishing detailed methods and adaptations across scientific disciplines.
- [Nature Methods](https://www.nature.com/nmeth/) — Research journal focused on new and improved life science methods; useful for method selection.

## Molecular Biology and General Laboratory Techniques

- [Addgene Protocols](https://www.addgene.org/protocols/) — Protocols for plasmid cloning, nucleic acid handling, and viral vector preparation.
- [NEB Protocols](https://www.neb.com/en-us/protocols) — Reagent-specific protocols for PCR, restriction digestion, DNA assembly, and sequencing library preparation.
- [Thermo Fisher Scientific Protocols](https://www.thermofisher.com/us/en/home/references/protocols.html) — Protocols for nucleic acid analysis, cell culture, transfection, and protein research.
- [QIAGEN Knowledge & Support](https://www.qiagen.com/us/knowledge-and-support) — Kit handbooks, protocols, and troubleshooting for nucleic acid purification and molecular analysis.
- [Takara Bio Support](https://help.takarabio.com/) — Product-specific guidance and troubleshooting for PCR, cloning, and sequencing workflows.
- [Promega Protocols](https://www.promega.com/resources/protocols/) — Technical manuals and protocols for molecular biology, reporter assays, and cell-based assays.

## Protein Analysis, Imaging, and Flow Cytometry

- [Bio-Rad Learning Center](https://www.bio-rad.com/en-us/r/lc) — Technical guides and workflows for electrophoresis, western blotting, PCR, and other laboratory methods.
- [Abcam Protocols](https://www.abcam.com/en-us/technical-resources/protocols) — Protocol library for antibody-based assays and related laboratory techniques.
- [Cell Signaling Technology Protocols](https://www.cellsignal.com/learn-and-support/protocols) — Protocols for western blotting, immunostaining, flow cytometry, and chromatin assays.
- [BD Biosciences Protocols](https://www.bdbiosciences.com/en-us/resources/protocols) — Protocols for flow cytometry sample preparation, staining, and cellular assays.

## Cell Culture

- [ATCC Culture Guides](https://www.atcc.org/resources/culture-guides) — Guides to growing, maintaining, and cryopreserving cell lines and microbial cultures.

## Sequencing, Single-Cell, and Spatial Assays

- [Illumina Support Center](https://support.illumina.com/) — Product documentation for sequencing library preparation, instrument operation, and troubleshooting.
- [10x Genomics Support](https://www.10xgenomics.com/support) — Product-specific protocols for sample preparation, single-cell assays, and spatial assays.

## Literature Search and Full-Text Archives

- [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) — Biomedical literature search for citations and abstracts, with links to available full text.
- [PubMed Central (PMC)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/) — Free full-text archive of biomedical and life science articles, including methods and protocol papers.
- [Europe PMC](https://europepmc.org/) — Life science literature search covering publications, preprints, and available full text.
